# Front-end map
- `app.js`: router, nav and page cache. Desktop menu is icons-only and opens almost instantly on hover (120ms) and pushes the page aside (never covers it), closing 150ms after the pointer leaves or on a click in the page (`navPeek`); Ctrl/⌘+\\ toggles it. The open menu is a cobalt blob (`.sidebar::before`) that grows out of the icon strip and shrinks back into it: one animated number `--nav-o` (registered with `@property`) drives the blob's clip, the menu's clickable area and the icon/text colours, and nothing in the menu moves between folded and open. The page follows the blob's edge via `--nav-c` on `.content`. No fading. Phones keep the top bar.
  - Page cache: KEEPABLE hashes stay in the DOM for 3 minutes and are cleared on any write.
  - Also: overview (`renderOverview`), campaign page (`renderCampaign`: Report/Payouts tabs switch in place, share dialog, ⋯ menu), campaign form, folders.
- Pop-ups: one app-wide handler (`closePopups` in app.js) closes them on an outside click or Escape. Menus/dropdowns built as `<details>` must be listed in `POPUP_DETAILS`; button-opened panels get `data-pop` and `aria-controls` on their button. Modal `<dialog>`s close on a click on the dimmed backdrop.
- Artist banners (`.art-band`) are rounded floating cards; on the campaign page the details card overlaps the banner's bottom.
- `report.js`: charts and post wall, shared with the client view. Charts show all four metrics by default and start at zero ("Posted"/"Start") so a line appears from the first check. The video preview drives TikTok's embed with `controls=0` and its postMessage API (own play/seek/mute bar), autoplays muted, and crops TikTok's header and like/comment/share column out of view (iframe enlarged and shifted, see styles.css); switching tabs never reloads the video.
- `clients.js`: Clients section (see Data model).
- `todo.html` + `todo.js` + `todo.css`: personal to-do BETA. One file, two places: the page `todo.html`, and inside the OS via `mountTodoInOS(sb, user)` (called from `route()` in app.js after sign-in; `unmountTodoInOS()` on sign-out). In the OS: a white round button bottom-right (`.td-fab`, cobalt to-do icon; the back-to-top button moves up above it via `body.has-todo`) opens a right-hand sidebar (`.td-drawer`, 480px, Escape / × / backdrop close). It only appears if the `todo` function's `config` answers (beta allowlist). It reloads every 10 min; when the Friday recap or Monday overview is waiting, the button gets a pulsing ring + blue dot and opening it goes straight into that flow. Everything renders into one panel (`root`), with `HOST` saying where it runs, one view at a time with slide/fade transitions: `setup` (first open: pick focus areas, then letters), `list` (on `todo.html` at 1100px+ wide: a board with every area as a column side by side, grouped by priority (Sam: CC + C = 1, S = 2, O = 3), each column with its own add box; narrower and in the OS sidebar: folder chips per area, composer with letter + EOD/EOW parsing, collapsible AI box), `task` (slide-in editor, autosaves: area, when = Today·EOD / Tomorrow / EOW / This week / Next week / a day + optional time, urgent switch, status, notes), `snap` (photo review), `recap`, `plan`, `link`. No pop-up windows inside the to-do except the OS confirm for switching a link off; removing a task has Undo. "Select" (next to the week line) turns rows into checkboxes with a bar at the bottom (Select all, Done, Remove: one `apply` call, Undo restores each task's old status; Escape leaves it); "Clear done tasks" under Done this week removes those in one go. Removing = status `dropped`, never a hard delete. A private link `todo.html#k=<key>` is stored in that browser (`stellar-todo-key`) and the hash is removed; otherwise it uses the OS session in the same browser. Friday recap = 4 steps (Done & partly done, Moving/carrying over, Urgent before the week ends, Not touched) then "Close the week"; Monday overview = carried over, add more (text or photo), "Popped up while away" placeholders, "Start the week". ⋯ menu has "Try … now" previews that change nothing. List numbers (urgent, this week, later) are what the AI box refers to.
- `chat.js`: team chat on the campaign page (`mountCampaignChat` from `renderCampaign`'s onPosts; `unmountCampaignChat` in `route()` when leaving the campaign's Report/Payouts, and on sign-out). Docked on the right at 1100px+ (`body.cc-open .content { margin-right: var(--cc-w) }`, 360px): the dashboard stays scrollable and clickable; below 1100px it comes in over the page and recap taps close it. Closes with ×, Escape, or a click on empty page space (Sam's ask); clicks on anything interactive on the dashboard (`INTERACTIVE` selector in chat.js) keep it open. Clean look: header "Chat" + DRI/runners, bubbles (own = cobalt, right), no avatars except who's here. Opened from "Team chat" in the banner or the edge tab (`#cc-tab`), with a badge = recap lines + live messages; open/closed is remembered (`stellar-chat-open`). Top: "Since your last visit" recap from RPC `campaign_recap(campaign, since)` (new videos by folder and who added them, views gained, edits per person or "the sheet", payouts added/moved/removed, invoices, folders, client links, new messages; the viewer's own actions left out; first visit = last 7 days; shown folded as one line pinned above the conversation, e.g. "Since Thursday 14:02 · 25 new videos · +2.9M views · 7 updates · 2 messages", unfolds into the lines, which jump to that part of the page). Then messages (`campaign_messages`, Realtime + 20 s poll fallback, @mentions with autocomplete from `team_members`, links, delete own = `deleted_at`), a "New since your last visit" divider, and who else has the campaign open (Realtime presence). `campaign_visits` (campaign, email, last_seen_at) is set when the page opens, when the tab is hidden and when leaving. `campaign_activity` is filled by triggers on campaigns (field list), payout_rows, invoice_requests, campaign_folders, share_links; actor = JWT email (empty = sheet sync/automatic); logging never blocks a save.
- `decks.js`: Decks section (#/decks, #/decks/<id>): deck list, links (on/off, expiry, and what each link asks for: No email / Email / Email + code, a dropdown per link that can be changed any time (`GATES`, `gateOf`; sets `require_email` + `verify_email`); stricter applies at once because `deck-view` only accepts a saved pass without a code when the link doesn't need one), views with seconds per slide, upload for missing videos, Figma button setup and keys, token countdown.
- `creators.js`: the campaign page's third tab "Creators" (#/c/<id>/creators; Report / Payouts / Creators): settings (drafts before posting, client approves drafts too, brief, message template with {handle} {name} {artist} {song} {folder} {steps} {link}), Drafts review (signed 1 h video links; Approve → approved, or team_ok when client review is on; Ask for changes needs a note; "Approve without waiting for the client"), and Creator pages (everyone on the payout sheet or in a folder; folder per creator; Copy link, Copy links for selected, WhatsApp (wa.me with the text; asks the number once and saves it on the card), Instagram/TikTok (message copied, chat opened), Email (mailto)). Nothing is sent or stored by the OS. `#app` gets `camp-on-payouts` for both team tabs and `data-camp-view` for the current one.
- Campaign Overview side panel: "Budget vs spent" box (tracker budget vs `campaign_summary.spend` = payouts + creator costs when there is any, else the sheet's Price vs Campaign Cost), and the Margin figure follows actual spend when the tracker has it.
- `campaign-tools.js`: "Duplicate…" dialog (in the campaign ⋯ menu and the overview menu; RPCs `duplicate_campaign_preview` / `duplicate_campaign`) and the wrap-up card `#wrapup` under `#mflags` (RPCs `campaign_wrapup` / `campaign_wrapup_tick`; `campaigns.wrapup`). Team only.
- `mywork.js`: "My work" (`#/me`): what needs the signed-in person now on the campaigns they run (DRI or runner): sections "Needs you now", "Invoice requests to handle", "Unread alerts" and "My campaigns". A section only shows when it has something. Uses `money.js` flags and the alert helpers from `notify.js`.
- `notify.js`: the alerts bell in the menu (unread count, panel of the latest 50 alerts). Click = mark read and open the link (only in-app `#/…` links are followed, `safeLink`). Live through Realtime on the person's own rows, with a 60 s check as fallback.
- `roster.js`: "Work with us" block on the creator card (from `creator_scorecard`, team only) and a "Cost per 1K views" sort on the roster.
- `invoice-plan.js`: the Invoice plan card on the campaign page (`#invplan`, between `#mflags` and `#wrapup`): parts with status, Request now (opens the request dialog with that part; after the insert the part is linked, and if linking fails the new request is cancelled), Edit/Add a part, Split… (with preview). The new-campaign form has an "Invoicing" choice (One invoice / In parts) that makes the plan right after creating the campaign. The request dialog has a "For" select with the plan's open parts.
- `analytics.js`: a "Reset filters" button (same pill as Home) appears once any filter differs from its default; clears `sessionStorage` `an-filters`.
- `dialog.js`: `confirmDialog` now also works inside an open modal `<dialog>` (it attaches there, so it isn't hidden behind it). Still no `confirm()` or `prompt()`.
- `payouts.js`, `invoices.js`, `analytics.js` (tabs, zoom routes `#/analytics/<kind>/<value>`), `trending.js`, `roster.js`, `discover.js`.
- Tests are throwaway Playwright scripts with mocked REST, kept in the session scratchpad and not in the repo. Check every change at 1440px and 390px for zero horizontal overflow and zero page errors.
