This policy explains what personal data Stellar collects when you open a deck or a campaign report we've shared with you, why we collect it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

We are the data controller for the data described below.

What we collect when you view a deck

  • Your email address, when you enter it to open the deck (or when we sent the link to you personally).
  • How you viewed the deck: when you opened it, which slides you looked at, how long each one was on screen while the page was in view, and which links in the deck you opened.
  • Basic device details: device type (phone, tablet or computer), browser, operating system, your time zone and approximate country.

We do not store your IP address. To stop abuse, we briefly keep a scrambled, one-way version of it that can't be turned back into the address.

Campaign report links don't ask for your email, and we don't record who views them.

Why we collect it

We use this data to understand which parts of our pitch interest you, so we can follow up with you in a relevant way, and to improve our decks. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in following up on the business material we've sent you (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). We don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or combine it with data from other websites.

How long we keep it

We keep viewing data for up to 24 months after your last visit, then delete it. You can ask us to delete it sooner at any time.

Cookies and storage

The deck page sets no cookies and loads no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers. If you enter your email, your browser keeps a small signed note for this one deck so you don't have to enter it again. It stays on your device for up to 180 days, and you can remove it by clearing your browser's site data.

Who handles the data for us

  • Supabase stores the data and runs our backend, on servers in the EU (Ireland).
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) serves the web page itself. Like any web host, it handles your IP address when it delivers the page.

Where a provider may access data from outside the EU/EEA, the transfer is covered by the EU Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it. You can also object to how we use it. Email soren@stellarmusic.dk, and we'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet), datatilsynet.dk.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
